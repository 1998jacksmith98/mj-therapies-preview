Privacy policy

How personal information is handled in connection with this website.

Who is responsible

Morgan Jolley / MJ Therapies is responsible for this website. Bookings are made through the clinic systems at the location you choose (ANA Therapies or Moreno Osteopathy). Those clinics act as separate data controllers for the booking and clinical records they hold.

What this site collects

This website does not currently have an on-site contact form that stores personal data. If you email Morgan directly, the information you send is used only to reply to your enquiry. Basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) may be processed by the hosting provider for security and performance.

Bookings

When you book, you are taken to the clinic’s own booking system. Please review that clinic’s privacy information for how they handle your contact details, appointment data and clinical notes. MJ Therapies does not receive or store your card details.

Cookies

This site uses only essential cookies required for basic functionality. It does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by MJ Therapies. For clinical records held by a clinic, contact that clinic directly. To contact Morgan about this policy: mjtherapiesinfo@gmail.com.

Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
