More about me

A bit more background on my training and how I work.

Qualifications

I graduated with a Bachelor of Osteopathy with Honours from Anglia Ruskin University in 2025. I’ve been practising sports massage for over 5 years.

Background

My background in sport and gymnastics coaching gives me a practical understanding of how people move and what the body is asked to do in training and everyday life.

Additional training

I have additional training in microcurrent therapy, visceral therapy, cupping, and taping.

Who I work with

I regularly work with athletes, children and people with everyday musculoskeletal complaints. The aim is always to understand what is driving the issue, then treat it in a way that fits the person in front of me.

Approach

I’m passionate about helping people of all ages and walks of life move better, feel stronger and get back to what they love. I look forward to supporting you on that journey.
