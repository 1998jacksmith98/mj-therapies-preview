How I work

Hands-on care.

  • Osteopathy
  • Sports massage
  • Pregnancy massage
  • Injury rehab and movement work
  • Posture and mobility

Every osteopath session is tailored to you - your body, your movement, your goals. From a thorough case history and movement assessment, to hands on treatment and clear aftercare advice, osteopathy looks at how your whole body works together, not just where it hurts. Whether you’re dealing with pain, tension or movement restriction, the aim is to help your body move better, stronger and more efficiently.

About

I’m Morgan.

I graduated with a Bachelor of Osteopathy in 2025 and have been practising in sports massage for over 3 years. With a background in sports, I’ve developed a deep appreciation for movement and the human body, which naturally led me to pursue osteopathy. I’m passionate about helping people from all ages and walks of life to achieve better health, mobility and pain management. I look forward to supporting each individual on their journey.
