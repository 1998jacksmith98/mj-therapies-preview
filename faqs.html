FAQs

A few things people usually ask before their first appointment.

What happens during my first osteopathy appointment?

We start with a proper case history — what’s been going on, how it affects you, and what you want back. I then look at how you move and carry out a physical assessment. Treatment is hands-on and tailored to what I find. We finish with clear aftercare so you know what to do between sessions.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing you can move in. Shorts or leggings and a top that can be adjusted are ideal so I can assess movement and treat the relevant areas. You can change at the clinic if you prefer.

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can book directly. Osteopaths are primary care practitioners, so a referral is not required. If you have relevant medical notes or imaging you are happy to share, bring them along.

How long does an appointment last?

Initial appointments are typically longer to allow time for the case history and assessment. Follow-up appointments are usually shorter. Exact times can vary by clinic — [please confirm typical new-patient and follow-up durations at each location].

What can osteopathy help with?

Osteopathy is often used for musculoskeletal problems such as back and neck pain, sports injuries, muscular tension, certain types of headaches, posture-related issues, and general aches from work, training or everyday life. It can also support people through pregnancy and postnatal recovery. Every plan is based on a proper assessment — there are no guarantees of outcome, and treatment is adapted to you.
